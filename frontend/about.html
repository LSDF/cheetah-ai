About Cheetah Cyber

Cheetah Cyber is an educational cybersecurity platform built to help people learn ethical hacking and security concepts the right way.

Advertisement Space (AdSense Ready)

Our Mission

We believe cybersecurity skills should be accessible. Our goal is to provide clear learning paths, practical challenges, and useful public OSINT tools so anyone can start learning without breaking the law or attacking real systems.

What We Offer

• Structured learning content
• Educational hacking challenges (CTF style)
• Public OSINT tools (username, WHOIS, DNS, IP)
• Clear focus on legal and ethical practice

Rules of the Platform

• Use everything here for educational purposes only
• Never attack systems you do not own or have permission to test
• Respect privacy and all applicable laws
• Do not use the OSINT tools for stalking, harassment, or illegal activity
